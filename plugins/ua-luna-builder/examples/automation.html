Automation lane

The breakpoints on the first track's Volume lane, drawn live from track.gain.automation. Every button is one SDK call and one LUNA undo entry, and the lane redraws from the live list — so a breakpoint you add, move or delete in LUNA itself appears here without a refresh. The mode selector calls track.setAutomationMode().

Note: the lane is addressed by tick, not by breakpoint object: move(fromTick, toTick), setValue(tick, value) and clear(startTick, stopTick). So “delete the first” removes every breakpoint sitting on that tick, which matters once two share one. The value axis is drawn from each breakpoint's stored value, normalised to the lane's own range.
