Edit clips and fades

Every track's active playlist as a lane, drawn from LUNA's compiled clips, so where clips overlap you see the one that plays (fact 116). Drag a clip to move what shows of it, as in LUNA: a clip that carries on under another is split at that edge if it shows again further along, and trimmed to it if not (fact 119). Drag a clip's edges to trim it, and the handles on its top corners to set its fade-in and fade-out. Where two clips meet, the handle on the edit sets a centred crossfade. Click to select a clip and place the cursor; the panel below does the rest. Each edit is one undo step in LUNA (fact 117), and the lanes follow edits made in LUNA itself.
