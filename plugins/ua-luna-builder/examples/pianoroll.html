Piano roll

A read-only piano roll over one MIDI clip of the open session, from clip.midi. It is live: notes created, edited, muted or deleted in LUNA (or by another client) appear without a refresh, because each edit arrives as a delta on the clip's MIDI table (fact 40). Positions are beats from the clip's start (fact 41).
