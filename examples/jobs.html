Bounce and export

Pick channels and a bar range, then bounce them through LUNA's render engine, or run LUNA's own main-bus export. Renders are objects under /renders with live state and progress (fact 44), so every render — including ones started in LUNA itself — appears in the table, and any of them can be aborted or deleted from here.

A bounce without output paths adds its audio to the session as new sources. An offline render reports no time remaining, because it runs faster than real time.
