Timeline

Each lane draws what it plays: its compiled clips, where an overlap is already resolved so a clip on a higher layer hides the one beneath (fact 116). Each segment's overview pairs are placed through the tempo map — fine pairs when zoomed in, coarse when zoomed out (fact 72), in the track's colour. A time-stretched clip draws LUNA's render; a clip whose render is not announced yet draws an outline only. Fades draw as ramps over the segment, crossfades in violet; the ramp is straight whatever the fade's shape. Nothing here is PCM, and missing analysis draws nothing rather than silence.
